Dayloop Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-28

Dayloop is a habit tracker that works without an account. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, and which third-party services are involved.

Publisher: Tegribas Global Limited, Nigeria. Contact: admin@tegribas.com

Short version

  • Your habits, check-ins, notes-free history and settings are stored only on your device.
  • There is no account, no login and no Dayloop server.
  • The free version shows ads (Google AdMob) on the Stats screen only.
  • Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play and RevenueCat.
  • We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data.

What stays on your device

Dayloop keeps the following in a local database on your phone: habit names, icons and colors, schedules and targets, check-ins, freeze days, reminder times, quiet hours, and app settings (theme, week start, haptics and similar). We never receive this data. It leaves your device only if you choose to export it (see below).

Your device's own backup (iCloud or Google backup) may include Dayloop's local data, according to your device backup settings. Dayloop has no cloud sync of its own.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Dayloop asks for notification permission only when you choose to turn on reminders. No push service is used and no notification data is sent to us. You can change this permission in your device settings at any time.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows a banner ad on the Stats screen. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google and its partners may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

  • In regions where consent is required (for example the EEA and UK), Dayloop shows a Google consent form before loading ads. You can reopen it from Settings, Ads privacy options.
  • On iOS, Dayloop asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before showing personalized ads. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized, and every feature keeps working.
  • Dayloop Pro removes ads. Ad services are not loaded for Pro users.

Purchases (Dayloop Pro)

Dayloop Pro is an optional subscription. Payment is processed by Apple or Google; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and check whether Pro is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription status, and basic device and app information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Your Pro status is cached on your device so the app works offline for a limited time.

Analytics

Dayloop contains a small analytics layer designed to record anonymous event counts only, never habit names or free text, with an opt-out toggle in Settings. In the current version no analytics service is connected, so no analytics data is sent anywhere. If that changes, this policy will be updated before the change ships.

Export and import

If you export your data (CSV or JSON, Pro feature), the file is created on your device and handed to the system share sheet. Where it goes afterward is up to you and the app you share it with. Importing a backup uses the system file picker and reads only the file you select. Dayloop does not request general storage access.

Deleting your data

Use Settings, Data, Reset all data to erase everything Dayloop stores on your device. Uninstalling the app also removes it (device backups excepted). Data held by Google (ads) or RevenueCat is subject to those companies' policies; contact us or them to ask about it. Subscriptions are cancelled in your App Store or Google Play account.

Permissions the app requests

  • Notifications (iOS and Android 13+): for habit and weekly review reminders.
  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS): only for personalized ads in the free version.
  • Android also declares permissions to restore reminders after a restart and to vibrate for haptic feedback.
  • Advertising ID (Android): used by the ads SDK in the free version.

Children

Dayloop is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
