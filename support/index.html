Dayloop support

Need help with Dayloop? Email admin@tegribas.com and we will reply as soon as we can. Please include your device model and app version (Settings, About).

Common questions

How do I restore my Pro purchase? Open Settings, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are managed by the store. On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions.

Where is my data stored? Only on your device. Dayloop has no account and no server. Use Settings, Data, Export (Pro) to keep a copy.

How do I delete my data? Settings, Data, Reset all data. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

I found a bug or have an idea. Email us. We read everything.
