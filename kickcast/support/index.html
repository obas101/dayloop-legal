Common questions

How do I restore my Pro purchase? Open Settings, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription? On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

How are predictions made? A statistical model (Poisson and Dixon-Coles style) computes the probabilities from past results. AI only writes the short explanation. Picks are saved before kickoff and graded against the real score, and the Results tab shows that record.

Are predictions guaranteed? No. They are for information and entertainment only.

Where is my data stored? On your device. Kickcast has no account.
