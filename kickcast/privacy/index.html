Kickcast Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-09

Kickcast shows football match predictions. It works without an account. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, and which third-party services are involved.

Publisher: Tegribas Global Limited, Nigeria. Contact: admin@tegribas.com

Short version

  • No account, no login. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data.
  • Your settings, favourite leagues and cached predictions stay on your device.
  • The app downloads the prediction feed from our server. Like any web request, this exposes your IP address to our hosting provider (Cloudflare) in standard server logs. We do not link it to you.
  • The free version shows banner ads (Google AdMob) on the Results screen only.
  • Purchases are handled by the App Store and RevenueCat.

What stays on your device

Kickcast stores your app settings, favourite leagues, onboarding state, a cached copy of the latest predictions and your Pro status locally. We never receive this data.

Prediction feed

Predictions are produced by a statistical model on our server from public fixture and results data. The app requests that feed over HTTPS. No personal information is sent with the request.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows a banner ad on the Results screen, provided by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect data such as your device advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

  • Where consent is required (for example the EEA and UK), Kickcast shows a Google consent form before loading ads.
  • On iOS, Kickcast asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before showing personalised ads. If you decline, ads are not personalised and every feature keeps working.
  • Kickcast Pro removes ads. Ad services are not loaded for Pro users.

Purchases (Kickcast Pro)

Kickcast Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription. Payment is processed by Apple; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and check whether Pro is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription status, and basic device and app information. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

No gambling

Kickcast provides information and entertainment only. It does not accept bets or offer gambling, and predictions are not guaranteed.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything Kickcast stores on your device. Subscriptions are cancelled in your Apple ID settings. To ask about data held by Google or RevenueCat, contact us or them.

Children

Kickcast is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above.
